How I Lead

Engineer by foundation. Leader by experience.

Technical credibility is not a hobby I left behind. It is how I hire, how I set standards, and how I earn the right to ask teams for excellence.

Build the organization

I am particularly strong at identifying and hiring excellent technologists, then developing them into teams that can carry real responsibility.

Align on outcomes

I create alignment around what the business actually needs, then drive teams through completion instead of leaving work stranded in process.

Air cover with accountability

I protect engineering teams from unnecessary organizational noise, competing priorities, and friction so they can solve the problem—while still holding the line on results.

Trust, kept

I build long-term professional relationships. Trust is foundational. When I make a commitment, I take keeping my word extremely seriously.

Leaders should create clarity, remove friction, develop people, and accept accountability for outcomes.
